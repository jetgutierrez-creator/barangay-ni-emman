Barangay Ni Emman Complaint Portal

Report local problems in your barangay safely and clearly. Your submission helps barangay officials respond faster to community concerns.

How it works

Use this form to describe the issue in your area, such as road damage, garbage, streetlight problems, drainage, safety concerns, or public service needs.

  • Provide your name and barangay location.
  • Choose the problem category that fits best.
  • Describe the issue clearly with location details.
  • Submit the form to record your complaint.

Useful reminders

Please provide accurate location and contact details so the barangay can follow up. If you prefer to remain anonymous, leave the contact field blank.

This page stores form submissions locally in your browser for demonstration purposes only; build a backend to send complaints to your barangay office in a real project.
